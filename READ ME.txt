RANK CHECKER

Quick Google top 10 checks, run from a dashboard in your browser.

TO USE
  Mac:      double-click "Rank Checker.command"
  Windows:  double-click "Rank Checker.bat"

The dashboard opens in your browser. Enter keywords (one per line) and your
domain, then click Run check. Results appear as each keyword finishes, and
your keywords and domain are remembered for next time.

COMPARING WITH WINCHER
  1. In Wincher, open Keyword Overview and click Export (CSV or Excel).
  2. In Rank Checker, choose "Compare with Wincher" and drop the file in.
  3. Tick the keywords to check (or use the Top 10 / Positions 11 to 20
     buttons), then click Check.
  The results show Wincher's position next to the manual one, biggest gaps
  first. Comparison checks look down to position 20, so allow about 45
  seconds per keyword.

A small window stays open while Rank Checker runs. Close it to shut down.

FIRST TIME ONLY
  You need Python 3.9 or newer: https://www.python.org/downloads/
  (On Windows, tick "Add python.exe to PATH" during install.)
  The first launch then takes a minute or two to set itself up.

  Mac: if it says the file can't be opened, right-click it, choose Open,
  then Open again. You only need to do this once.

GOOD TO KNOW
  - A Chrome window opens during a check. Leave it open.
  - If Google shows a CAPTCHA, solve it in that Chrome window and the check
    carries on. If Google blocks the check, wait an hour or so.
  - Each keyword takes about 30 seconds. Keep checks to 30 keywords or fewer.
  - Results are saved in the "results" folder.
